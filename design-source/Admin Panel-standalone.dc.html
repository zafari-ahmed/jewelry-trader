AI & Automation

Not active — Phase 1
Master AI switch — off
No AI capability is available in Phase 1. This section is configured and ready; turning the master switch on will be possible once the provider is provisioned. Nothing here is broken.
Attribute auto-fill
Suggest metal, period and stone data from intake photos. Suggestions enter the record yellow and stay inactive until a human accepts them.
Customer-facing descriptions
Draft storefront copy from the verified attribute set. Requires reviewer approval before publishing.
Comparable-sale pricing
Suggest a retail band from auction comparables. Never sets price directly.
Editable once the master switch is enabled in Phase 2.
How suggestions will behave
A suggested value lands yellow and is excluded from listings, POS and reports until reviewed.
Accepting a suggestion turns the field green and attributes it to the reviewer.
Editing a suggestion instead of accepting it turns the field blue — a human override.
This mapping is already implemented in the inventory form, so no screen changes are needed when AI is switched on.
